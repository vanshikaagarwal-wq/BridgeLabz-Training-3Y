The Importance of Good Typography

Good typography makes written content easier to read and understand. It improves the visual appearance of a webpage and creates a better reading experience for users.

Clear typography helps readers focus on important information. Proper spacing and a suitable font size also improve readability.

Designers often use different font styles to create emphasis and make important words stand out from normal text.

Balanced text alignment and consistent spacing create a clean and professional webpage layout.
